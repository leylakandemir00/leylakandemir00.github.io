About

I am a Research Professional in the Department of Economics at the University of Chicago, where I work with Professor Ali Hortaçsu on US electricity markets and the economic effects of AI infrastructure growth. I completed my MA in Economics at Chicago in June 2026 as a Fulbright Scholar from Turkey, after a BA in Economics at Boğaziçi University.

I am applying to PhD programs in economics for entry in Fall 2027. I am interested in how large infrastructure investments, such as data centers, change prices and competition in energy markets, and in applying modern empirical methods to questions in industrial organization.

Outside of research, I love live music. My favorite piece is Beethoven's Seventh Symphony, here in Carlos Kleiber's 1976 recording with the Vienna Philharmonic.
